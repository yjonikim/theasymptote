How do you say what you mean?

Editorial thought partnership for writers whose ideas outrun their drafts — essays, manuscripts, talks, and applications. Let me help you close the distance.

Method

Excavation before markup.

You can feel the vibe of what you're thinking, but you're convinced there's no way to give that vibe a shape. That concretization is where I can help most. I ask the questions that identify the roots of your ideas so that we can give them structure and punch.

Every engagement starts with diagnosis: a memo on what the piece is doing, what it wants to do, and the distance between the two. How we dialogue from there is up to you: in writing, by call, or even in person. Only once we share a vision do we get involved in revision and rehaul, line work, and polishing.

For some clients, I can also hold the pen on your behalf. After enough excavation, I write the first draft in your voice, and you steer us in the refinement. The authorship stays yours; I only ever put on the page what our dialogue surfaced.

I'm someone engaged in writing about the hard work of thinking and knowing, so I can meet your argument on the merits, not just its grammar. I welcome a challenge.

Services
Ongoing thought partnership
Monthly work on whatever your brain is building, whether a single big project or ongoing series. This is dialogue-intensive by design; reaching a mutual understanding of your sensibility, convictions, and intent is what lets your voice come through on the page.
Single pieces
A diagnostic memo and call on one essay, talk, manuscript, or application. It’s also the best way to find out whether we think well together before committing to anything longer.
College applications
Editing and coaching for high-school applicants. This can range from editing existing drafts to guiding you from scratch. We talk through the story you want to tell before structuring and refining it into your final statement.
